Field manual

Goal. Destroy the enemy base. Medieval always holds the left, Modern the right.
Gold. Both sides start with 100 gold and earn 2.5 gold / second. Every enemy troop you kill pays a bounty of 40% of its hiring cost (a 50g Police Officer = 20g). Self-destructs give no bounty to the enemy.
Training. Open the Shop (B) and click a troop (search box and list/card view at the top). Your 3 most recently hired troops get quick-hire keys (1 2 3) while the shop is open. Bought troops queue up (max 5) and walk out of your base. Click a queued troop to cancel it for a full refund. Army limit: 40.
Skill Tree. (N, left of the Barracks) Every kill by a troop type adds to that type's level for this match (5 kills to Lv 1, then 10, 15, 20…). Levels unlock evolutions, e.g. Viking → Frenzy or Lightning Viking at Lv 5. Evolving is free; every troop you hire afterwards comes out evolved (same price, same Barracks upgrades). Troops already on the field stay as they are.
Auto-charge. Fresh troops march at the enemy base and fight anything in their path. Your orders override this; C sends them charging again.
Melee vs ranged. Melee troops must touch their target. Ranged troops shoot from their range circle (select one troop to see it) — projectiles always hit, but they're fragile. In formations, ranged troops automatically stand behind melee.
Damage types. Physical is reduced by Armor, Magic is reduced by Ward, True ignores both. All current troops deal physical damage and have 0 Ward. Each hit rolls ±1 damage.
Armor & Ward scale like Dota: reduction = 0.06×D ÷ (1 + 0.06×D). 2 → 10.7%, 5 → 23%, 10 → 37.5%, 20 → 54.5%. Every point helps, with diminishing returns; negative values make a troop take extra damage.
Spawn shield. Every troop is invulnerable for 5 seconds after it leaves the base (white bubble): it can be targeted but takes no damage and can't be crowd-controlled. The bubble blinks before it pops.
HP regen restores health per second, even mid-fight.
Evasion & miss (Dota-style). Evasion is a % chance to dodge an incoming attack; miss chance is a % chance your own attacks whiff. Abilities can't be dodged. Multiple evasion sources stack multiplicatively (30% + 30% = 51%).
Abilities. A troop can have up to 5 actives and any number of passives. Actives are cast with Q W E R T (1st, 2nd… active) or by clicking the icon.
Attack speed is attacks per minute: 60 = one hit per second, 120 = two. DPS = damage × (1 − enemy reduction) × attack speed ÷ 60.
Tooltips. Select a troop and hover any ability icon in the bottom panel to see what it does and whether it's active right now.
Repair (H). Toggle to heal your base (+10 HP/s) while draining gold (5 gold/s). It only drains while the base is damaged and switches off when you run out of gold. Upgrade it with Repair Crews in the Barracks.
Barracks (V). Spend gold on upgrades for your troops and base: stat boosts, stronger passives, new abilities, wall towers, base HP and income. Each research takes time; you can queue up to 5 (the same upgrade several times too), and everything resets next match. Click a queued or running research to cancel it for a full refund. The AI researches too.
Inventory. Every troop has 6 item slots (select one troop to see them). Neutral creeps can drop items: select a troop and right-click the item to pick it up. Items give stats or passives; hover a slot to see the item. Items are lost when the carrier dies.
Battlefield. One wide lane, with forest (medieval side) and city back alleys (modern side) above and below it holding 4 neutral camps.
Day & night. 5 minutes of day, then 5 minutes of night, alternating (see the sun/moon timer at the top). At night torches and street lamps light the lane.
Obstacles. Trees, rocks, buildings, fences, barrels, lamps and torches are solid. Troops path around them, so the forest and the back alleys can only be entered through their openings.
River (low ground). The shallow river where the two sides meet is low ground. Ranged attacks from the river at a target up on the bank miss 50% of the time (melee is unaffected), and troops in the river can't see up onto the banks.
Fog of war. You only see what your troops and base can see. Base vision: 35 by day / 20 at night. Troops: 30 by day / 16 at night (Sniper +3 while prone). Each troop has its own vision value, so they can be tuned separately. Trees and buildings block sight. A troop that attacks is revealed to its victim's side for 1.5s.
Scouting. Clicking the enemy base shows only what you saw the last time you had vision on it (upgrades, research, towers, repair). Get vision again to update it.
Flying units. Hover above the ground, fly over trees and buildings and see over them. Melee can't hit them; ranged attacks against them miss 50% of the time.
Invisibility. Invisible enemies can't be seen or attacked unless something detects them (Sentry Wards are coming). Attacking or moving usually breaks it.
Break. A broken unit's passives stop working until it wears off (shown dark with a red slash). A few passives are unbreakable.
Coming later: buildable structures (turrets etc.), troops that benefit from day or night, weather.
